About

Two jobs,
one mission.

By day, I'm a software engineer shipping production code and learning what it actually means to build things that scale. By night (and weekends), I run a panel community for early-career students in tech, create content focused on making the hardest parts of tech careers actually approachable, and build tools that give my community a real edge.

I started hosting panels because I couldn't find the room I needed, so I built it. 500+ students later, I also built the Recruiting Buddy Initiative to give students the one-on-one support that panels alone can't provide. I'm still convinced that access and community change everything. Tech doesn't have to feel like an exclusive club. I'm here to prove it.
